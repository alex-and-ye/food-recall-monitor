The product, la houssaye lhous pt l eveque aop cru 400, is from France. This dairy product is being recalled due to the detection of Listeria monocytogenes, which can cause listeriosis, a serious disease that may lead to vomiting, diarrhea or constipation, headaches, fever, meningitis or septicemia. Consumers are advised not to consume this product and to report it to the point of sale for destruction.
