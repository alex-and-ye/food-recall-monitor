The product, cooked grey shrimp non-deboned jumbo MSC S-AT, is from Van Belzen BV - Distributor Zeelandia. This product is being recalled due to the presence of Listeria monocytogenes, which poses a risk of listeriosis, a serious disease that can have an incubation period of up to eight weeks and may cause fever, headaches, muscle aches, neurological complications, and maternal-fetal damage in pregnant women. Consumers are advised not to consume or use the product and to contact the consumer service instead.
