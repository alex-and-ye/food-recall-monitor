Cooked grey shrimp non-deheaded jumbo MSC S-AT is being recalled from Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Occitanie, Pays-de-la-Loire, and Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur due to the presence of Listeria monocytogenes, which can cause listeriosis; severe forms may include neurological complications and maternal or fetal impacts. Consumers who have consumed these products and are experiencing fever, headaches, muscle aches, or other symptoms should consult their general practitioner. Do not consume the product and contact customer service for further instructions.
