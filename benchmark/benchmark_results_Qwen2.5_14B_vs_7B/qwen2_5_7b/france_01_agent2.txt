The jumbo peeled white shrimp MSC S-AT is being recalled from the markets in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Occitanie, Pays-de-la-Loire, and Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur due to the presence of listeria monocytogenes, which can cause listeriosis, a serious disease. Consumers are advised not to consume or use the product and to contact customer service if they have consumed it and are experiencing symptoms such as fever, headaches, muscle aches, or other severe forms.
