The product is jumbo cooked grey shrimp non-deboned (MSC S-AT) distributed by Van Belzen BV - Distributor Zeelandia, originating from various regions including Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Occitanie, Pays-de-la-Loire, and Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. It is being recalled due to the presence of Listeria monocytogenes, posing a risk of listeriosis which can be severe, especially for pregnant women, immunocompromised individuals, and the elderly. Consumers should not consume the product and contact customer service for further instructions.
