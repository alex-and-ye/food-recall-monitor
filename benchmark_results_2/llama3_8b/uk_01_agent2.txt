The product is Shama Falooda Almond flavour drink, from Sunrise International Foods Ltd. It is being recalled because it contains milk which is not mentioned on the label, posing a health risk for anyone with an allergy to milk or intolerance to milk or milk constituents. Consumers who have bought this product and have an allergy to milk should not drink it; instead, please return it to the store where it was bought from for a full refund.
