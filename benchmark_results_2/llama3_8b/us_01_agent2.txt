The product is a ready-to-eat chicken Caesar wrap from Fresh & Ready Foods LLC, and it is being recalled due to misbranding and unreported allergens. The health risk is that the individual dressing packets contain fish (anchovies), a known allergen, that is not declared on the product label, posing a risk to individuals with fish allergies. Consumers are urged not to consume these products and offices are advised not to serve them; instead, they must be discarded or returned to the machine owners.
