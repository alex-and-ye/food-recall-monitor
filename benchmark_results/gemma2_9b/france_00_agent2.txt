This recall involves La Houssaye Pont l'Évêque AOP Cru 400g cheese produced in France. The product is being recalled due to the detection of Listeria monocytogenes, which can cause listeriosis, a serious illness with symptoms such as fever, headaches, muscle aches, and in severe cases, meningitis or septicemia. Consumers should not consume this product, return it to the point of sale, and contact the company at 0231206400 for further instructions.
