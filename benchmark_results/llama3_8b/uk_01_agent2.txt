Shama Falooda Almond flavour drink from Sunrise International Foods Ltd is being recalled. The product contains milk which is not mentioned on the label, posing a health risk for anyone with an allergy to milk or intolerance to milk or milk constituents. Consumers who have bought the product and have an allergy to milk should not drink it; instead, return it to the store where it was bought from for a full refund.
