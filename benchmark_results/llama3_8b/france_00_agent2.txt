The product, "La Houssaye" Pont l'Evêque du Lot 2610098 cheese, from France, is being recalled due to the detection of Listeria monocytogenes, which poses a risk of listeriosis, a serious disease that can cause vomiting, diarrhea or constipation, headaches, fever, meningitis or septicemia. Consumers are advised not to consume the product and to report it to the point of sale for refund or exchange options.
